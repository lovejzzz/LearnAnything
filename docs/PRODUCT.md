# Product contract

## Promise

LearnAnything maps what exists, discovers what a learner knows, and guides the learner toward what they want to become using free knowledge and demonstrated mastery.

The product answers five questions:

1. What does the destination actually require?
2. Which required capabilities has this learner demonstrated?
3. What is the smallest prerequisite-correct path from here to there?
4. What should the learner do next?
5. What evidence would justify moving forward?

## Complete relative to a goal

LearnAnything does not promise an exhaustive representation of human knowledge. A path is complete when, relative to its declared goal and depth:

- target capabilities are explicit;
- their prerequisite closure is represented;
- important alternative branches and disagreements are visible;
- each important capability has a learn-practice-demonstrate loop;
- sources and uncertainty are visible;
- the learner can demonstrate the target capabilities through authentic evidence;
- the map exposes meaningful continuation beyond the destination.

A complete path can take three days or three years. Duration is an estimate derived from the graph, learner state, pace, and evidence requirements—not a fixed property of a topic.

## Primary user journey

1. **Declare a destination.** The learner describes what they want to understand or become capable of doing, why, and how deeply.
2. **Clarify the goal.** The product turns a vague topic into target capabilities, constraints, and optional branches.
3. **Locate the learner.** A short diagnostic and prior evidence identify demonstrated, uncertain, and missing capabilities.
4. **See the territory.** The learner sees the whole relevant map, their current frontier, the destination, major branches, and unknown areas.
5. **Receive a route.** The planner computes a prerequisite-closed path and explains why each stage exists.
6. **Work one horizon.** The interface emphasizes the next useful week rather than dumping a multi-year syllabus on the learner.
7. **Demonstrate mastery.** The learner solves, builds, explains, analyzes, creates, or performs.
8. **Replan.** Evidence updates learner state and the planner recalculates the path without rewriting history.

## Product principles

### Map first, path second, lesson third

The global graph describes the territory. A path is a learner-specific view. A lesson is one temporary experience on that path. Never reverse this ownership.

### Capability over consumption

Watching, reading, clicking, and time-on-page are activity signals. They are not evidence that a learner can understand or use something.

### Complete map, small horizon

Expose the long journey so the learner can orient and choose. Emphasize only the next tractable set of actions.

### Free by architecture

The core product requires no paid API, generative model, account, or proprietary curriculum. Popular graphs are reusable shared assets rather than regenerated per learner.

### Source visible

The product distinguishes sourced claims, community judgment, learner evidence, deterministic inference, and AI proposals. It never presents generated prose as authority.

### Multiple legitimate paths

Different goals, traditions, modalities, and learner constraints can produce different valid routes through the same territory.

### Learner ownership

Progress and evidence are exportable. A learner can use the site without creating an account and can leave without losing their learning record.

## Domain examples

### Quantum physics

A conceptual goal may route through waves, probability, complex numbers at an intuitive level, measurement, and canonical experiments. A research goal requires substantial mathematics, classical mechanics, linear algebra, differential equations, formal quantum mechanics, computation, and specialist branches.

### Philosophy

The graph must represent arguments, primary texts, interpretive traditions, historical context, objections, and unresolved disagreement. It must not force one canonical prerequisite order where the discipline has several legitimate routes.

### Minecraft Redstone

The graph includes game mechanics, circuits, timing, logic, debugging, builds, and version/platform constraints. Mastery is demonstrated by functioning constructions and explanations, not quizzes alone.

These domains are permanent architecture tests because they resist a single school-course template.

## Non-goals for the first product

- Generating a textbook or complete course as prose.
- Replacing teachers, experts, communities, or primary sources.
- Hosting copied versions of arbitrary web content.
- Building an LMS, social network, credential marketplace, or AI tutor.
- Training or fine-tuning a model.
- Supporting every domain before the learning loop works in three domains.
- Predicting a precise completion date.
- Producing one universal quality or readiness score.

## Success measures

The first product should measure:

- whether the route contains the necessary prerequisites;
- whether learners understand why each stage exists;
- whether learners can demonstrate target capabilities;
- how often paths change after diagnostic evidence;
- time to the first meaningful action;
- return and continuation after one week;
- learner-reported confusion and missing prerequisites;
- resource and mastery-check usefulness;
- graph defects found across domains.

Engagement is useful only when connected to progress or understanding. Number of generated lessons, graph size, tokens, and internal conformance scores are not product outcomes.

## Open decisions

- Code license and knowledge/data license.
- Governance for accepting public graph changes.
- Whether accounts and cross-device sync belong after the local-first MVP.
- How expert and community review earn visible trust signals.
- Which accessibility and localization requirements are launch-blocking.
